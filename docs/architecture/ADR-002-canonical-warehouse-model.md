# ADR-002 · Canonical tournament warehouse

**Status** Accepted. Dennis approved the backend design on 2026-11-23.
**Date** 2026-11-23  
**Work order** [TASK-010](../../tasks/010-canonical-warehouse-model.md)

## Decision

Use one canonical evidence warehouse. Public and authenticated observations share logical entity keys and observation structures. Start the physical rollout with public evidence. Authenticated Tabroom is an expected source once TASK-003 production authorization is approved. Physical constraints, migrations and ingestion require separate approved work orders.

### Evidence-first principle

DebateOS stores evidence first and promotes conclusions only when the evidence supports them.

### Judge-centric intelligence

Track an entity when it can inform an actionable debate-round decision. The judge is the primary analytical focus because the competitive objective is to maximize the probability that the judge votes for the team. Judge-focused marts and dossiers can combine current stated preferences, assignment history, verified voting tendencies, squad and team matchup history (including SNU), verified Aff/Neg tendencies, and panel selection and judge-preference analysis. Expose freshness and evidence confidence. Preserve each normalized entity's own grain and keys. Use evidenced relationships to assemble judge views.

The [public inventory](../research/tabroom-public-data-inventory.md) and [authenticated discovery](../research/tabroom-authenticated-access.md) establish the source coverage. The current adapter's approved anonymous reads remain tournament REST, invite REST and structured bulk export. The field endpoint corroborates entry IDs but adds no runtime dependency.

## Entity and relationship catalog

Keys below are logical. Source identifiers are typed by entity and namespaced by provider. Child keys include their indicated owner scope. Preserve raw values and normalize only verified ID representations, such as an integral number and its equivalent decimal string. A scoped key can have many observations over time. A resolved relationship requires an explicit field or validated source containment, consistent scope, and one unambiguous target. Retain unresolved references and conflicting evidence separately from accepted links.

| Entity | Grain, logical key and relationships | Source and evidence |

### Identity resolution

Explicit Tabroom judge/person IDs and verified crosswalks are the strongest practical identity evidence for judges. Tabroom squad and school names provide context. Durable person and team identity requires independent evidence. An entry's debater membership can vary across tournaments. Keep roster membership entry-scoped and defer durable team identity until a season/squad construct can be supported by evidenced memberships. Dennis reports that each debater has an individual Tabroom account. Verify account identity and its relationship to student references in the source/API before using it as a crosswalk rule. College wiki records can supplement team identity where available. Coverage is incomplete because posting is voluntary.

Reserve debate decision and winner facts until side interpretation, explicit vote/decision, multi-judge aggregation, bye, forfeit and correction rules are verified. Event placement stays at result-set grain. Raw judging history labels and vote fields carry the same semantic limits.

## Season-scoped datasets

A season scope is a reusable dataset definition, such as `2026-27 college policy debate`. Membership comes from explicit event decisions in an approved manifest revision. Changes to dates, classification or membership create a successor revision and preserve the previous decisions. Judge eligibility, field and assignment analytics, and season standings analyses pin the same scope ID and version, along with their evidence cutoff and resolution policy. Only included events enter the dataset. Keep exclusions, unresolved events and discovery gaps visible for coverage reporting. Apply the same publication and access rules to membership evidence and analytical inputs.

## Snapshot, replay and serving policy

Prioritize tracing each observation to exact source evidence to support debugging and validation during early development. Retain authorized source bytes in scope-controlled storage when retention permits. Use a sanitized route and parameters plus a grant reference for protected captures. Keep credentials, cookies and contact data out of analytical rows and repository fixtures. A retry of the same capture operation reuses its capture identity. A later fetch gets its own timestamp and snapshot even when bytes match. Deduplicate immutable bytes only within the same access partition. Replay against the same snapshot and parser version is idempotent. Parser upgrades produce separately traceable observations. Fixture digests and pointers refer to fixture bytes. Source digests and pointers refer to original bytes.

Record capture outcomes independently of publication and entity state. Distinguish observed values, explicit empty responses, omitted fields, not requested, evidenced unpublished, unauthorized/expired, forbidden, ambiguous not-found, invalid response and transient failure. Carry verification states such as verified, unresolved and conflicting. A failed or narrower capture cannot erase a prior observation or prove withdrawal. As-of views use eligible evidence captured by the cutoff and a reviewed resolution policy. Retain contradictory observations and resolution history. Use explicit withdrawal evidence to suppress serving while retaining history subject to retention and deletion rules.

Public views use public-eligible evidence only, including every input to joins, aggregates, caches and exports. Record source authorization class, purpose, audience, grant scope and retention policy at observation/evidence grain. Serve protected inputs only to audiences permitted by the applicable policy. Check those at capture, storage, access and serving time. Joins and derived outputs inherit the most restrictive applicable input policies, including the intersection of audience permissions where required. A shared identifier alone conveys no access to a protected fact. Task 003 gates backend ingestion. Keep account/coach partitions isolated and use expiring/revocable access, auditable reads and access-aware cache keys.

### Live production enumeration

On November 23, 2026, the [production IndexCards OpenAPI v1.2.0](https://api.tabroom.com/v1) matched the approved pin, SHA-256 `e01682f296589c599f6390127ef06a6c9fb10f8c6ab3019a700727322bd7e375`. It advertises cookie-authenticated `GET /rest/paradigms` with optional `search`, `limit` (default 50, range 1–100) and `offset` (default 0). The response is an array of search matches. The contract provides no unfiltered enumeration guarantee, stable ordering, continuation token or total count. Three anonymous production requests returned 401 problem JSON: omitted search at offsets 0 and 1 with limit 1, and empty search at offset 0 with limit 1. Prior authenticated evidence is one numeric search for 366665 with limit 1 returning an empty array, plus successful person-keyed detail and history. **Search pagination is documented. Safe authenticated global listing remains unverified.** An anonymous 401 does not resolve authenticated behavior. Prerelease behavior supplies no production evidence.

If Tabroom confirms this use is supported, validate with a legitimately scoped session and single-row requests: compare omitted and empty search with a vendor-approved known-positive search at offset 0, compare offsets 0 and 1 and repeat offset 0 for stability. Record status, shape, row count and restricted comparison evidence. This can distinguish search behavior and detect an ignored offset. A complete enumeration claim also needs vendor-confirmed scope, filtering, stable traversal and permitted use. Even a working global listing would still need tournament-based eligibility.

### Restricted entities and version history

| Entity | Grain and key | History and serving |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Judge eligibility evidence | Manifest revision, event, scoped judge and evidence ID. Optional resolved person. | Keep roster and assignment basis, link resolution and provenance. Project distinct eligible persons per manifest revision and access partition from included events with evidenced panel assignments and an unambiguous person link. |
| Paradigm capture | Person ID plus capture ID, with snapshot ID, UTC time, source route, authorization class and grant scope. | Record outcome and pointers even if no content version was produced. Validate response person ID before attaching evidence. Repeated reads preserve observation history. |
| Paradigm version | Person ID, access partition and version ID. Where retention permits, preserve both a sanitized source-faithful representation that keeps meaningful source structure for fidelity and version history, and normalized text for authorized search, summarization and derived tendencies. Record a SHA-256 digest for each representation, the sanitization and normalization versions, source review/update dates when supplied, certification observations and supporting capture references. If text retention is prohibited, keep only permitted metadata and digests. | Each representation inherits the revision's authorization partition and traces to the supporting capture, including its snapshot, UTC timestamp, source route and grant scope. Unchanged captures link to the current revision. Changes to content, transformation versions, review metadata or certifications create an immutable successor with predecessor, first-seen capture and changed fields. Resolve successor links from immutable lineage and calculate first and last seen from matching captures. Reversions are timestamped transitions. Missing or denied captures do not overwrite revisions. |
| Certification observation | Per paradigm version and verified source ID or certification pointer. | Preserve source label, dates and values when exposed. |
| Judging history observation | First-class stream per verified person, with each row keyed by snapshot and source pointer. | Authenticated `GET /rest/paradigms/{personId}/record` returned judging-history rows in TASK-003. Preserve raw source labels, dates and votes independently of paradigm revisions. Promote verified side, decision and winner interpretations only through explicit evidence. |
| Derived tendency | Person ID, feature definition/version and derivation run ID, referencing exact input versions and evidence. | Record model/rule version, generation time, sample size and uncertainty. Carry the input access scope through serving, caches and exports. |

Paradigm text, certifications, history and derived outputs carry the authorization and retention policies of their source evidence. Retention does not authorize public serving. Both representations, indexes and derived outputs remain restricted to their permitted purpose and audience. Public republication, sharing, retention, text indexing and model processing each require applicable authorization. Preround dossiers can show an authorized judge's self-described preferences, review date and source evidence, plus separately labeled tendencies with sample size and confidence where evidence supports them. Public assignment analytics can still describe panel coverage and repeat assignments from public lineage. Treat self-description and verified historical behavior as different evidence. Defer outcome-based features such as SNU for/against records, per-squad tendencies, Aff/Neg bias and judge-team matchup outcomes until side, decision and winner semantics are verified. Cite the exact input observations and versions for every derived tendency. Revocation and authorized deletion must cover versions, indexes, caches and derived artifacts as applicable.
